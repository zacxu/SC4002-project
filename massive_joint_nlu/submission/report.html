1. Setup

MASSIVE 1.0 en-US with the official splits (11,514 / 2,033 / 2,974 utterances; 60 intents; 55 slot types, written as 108 BIO labels). The only embedding source is NLPL Word2Vec model 6 (300-d; 89.3% of training words covered); no pretrained language model is used. Every stored value stays below 20M. Selection uses dev only; test was scored once, after a lock file was written. The joint score is the mean of intent accuracy and exact-span slot micro-F1.

2. Stage 1 baseline

A 4-layer Transformer with 4.34M parameters, trained for 12 epochs (best epoch 11). Dev: intent 82.83, slot F1 66.09, joint 74.46. Test: 81.57 and 63.23. Slot F1 on the Slot-Train-OOV subset (a slot word unseen in training) is 29.9 on test, against 70.1 for utterances without an unseen word.

5. Error analysis (strict single, test)

967 of 2,974 utterances contain an error: 563 slot-only, 224 intent-only, 180 both. 63% of Slot-Train-OOV utterances contain an error, against 27% of utterances without an unseen word.

  • “i like senatra songs”: unseen misspelled artist tagged music_genre; the context word “songs” drives the type.
  • “play a retro song in my playlist”: the unseen word “retro” receives no tag.
  • “send a giggle my way”: predicted email_sendemail, not general_joke; the verb “send” outweighs the unseen word “giggle”.

6. What was rejected; verification

  • Rejected in the ladder: 80-epoch training (84.55 against 84.92); mention-replacement augmentation (84.71); the 12 random configurations (none beat the base).
  • Rejected in rung E: MC-dropout inference (at most +0.06); a train-only mention table (+0.02 strict, +0.34 flagged); a two-member ensemble (+0.59 over the single). Under the 0.8-point one-seed tie margin of the protocol, the simpler system was kept in each case.
  • Caveat: rungs C, D and R were adopted on any improvement of the 3-seed mean. Under the 0.8 margin used in rung E, D and R would be ties; read the final systems with that in mind.
  • Verification: four independent code reviews found an MC intent-mix bug, a seed-spread leak into the test split, a lock-ordering problem and a cap-counting issue. All were fixed before the one-time test. The notebook retrains the final systems and reproduces their dev numbers within one point.

7. Limitations and future work

  • Authorship: Stage 1 was developed with AI assistance. The group must replace it with its own independent Stage 1 before submission.
  • Evidence: dev-selected numbers are optimistic, and each final system has one training seed on test. Across dev seeds the strict family varies by ±0.17 intent and ±0.30 slot F1.
  • Ceiling: without a pretrained language model and under the 20M cap, the remaining errors are mostly unseen-word and ambiguous-intent cases.
  • Data: one BIO label (I-transport_type) occurs only in test, inherited from the hand-off. It is never a training target and has no dev effect. Data come from the verified CSV, not the official en-US.jsonl.
  • Future work: pointer-style span copying for unseen names; character-level span detection for misspellings; intent–slot consistency reranking.
